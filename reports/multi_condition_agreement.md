# Multi-condition operating-point agreement (task D5)

Generated by `scripts/multi_condition_agreement.py`; numbers from
`results/multi_condition_agreement.json`. Every figure here is regenerated, not
transcribed.

## Why more than one probe

The published agreement number is one DC operating point, at one bias, under one
set of placeholder component values. Three things could each be doing the work
instead of the circuit: the values, the bias, or the choice of a DC operating
point as the probe at all. At DC an inductor is a short and a capacitor an open,
so **every reactive element contributes nothing to the measurement** -- a circuit
whose only recovery error is in its L/C network scores a perfect 1.000 and is
counted among the successes. That is the same shape of blind spot as
`canonicalize_terminals` being unable to see a pin swap, and it deserved the same
treatment.

## Result 1 -- the values are not doing the work

Agreement was recomputed requiring exactness under **all 10 independent E24
value assignments**, each drawn from the 5% preferred series within ordinary
working decades and applied identically to both decks.

| criterion | exact rate [95% CI] | n |
| --- | --- | --- |
| under all 10 assignments | 0.3516 [0.2527, 0.4505] | 91 |
| under at least one | 0.3516 [0.2527, 0.4505] | 91 |

The two criteria are identical: **0 circuits pass a single assignment and
fail the all-10 criterion**. Agreement, and disagreement, are properties of the
recovered circuit and not of the numbers put into it.

## Result 2 -- the bias point is not doing the work either

| condition | informative | degenerate | mean F1 [95% CI] | exact rate [95% CI] |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `op_primary` | 92 | 1 | 0.6314 [0.5574, 0.7036] | 0.3370 [0.2391, 0.4348] |
| `op_low_bias` | 93 | 1 | 0.6229 [0.5499, 0.6945] | 0.3333 [0.2366, 0.4301] |
| `ac_1khz` | 51 | 101 | 0.6014 [0.4832, 0.7144] | 0.4118 [0.2745, 0.5490] |

*Degenerate* means the reference response is identically zero, so every
corresponded node agrees for free; those are excluded rather than banked.

`op_low_bias` is the same probe at DC 1 instead of DC 15. Over the
92 circuits both can see, **0 circuits change classification in one
direction and 0 in the other** (exact McNemar p = 1.000). A
fifteen-fold change in supply voltage moves nothing.

## Result 3 -- AC measures 32 circuits the DC probe cannot

The published metric is defined only where a DC operating point exists on both
sides: 92 circuits. A **linear** network that is singular at DC -- a
capacitor in series with the only source, say -- is perfectly well defined at
1 kHz, and `.ac` is then a direct complex-linear solve needing no operating point.

| | circuits |
| --- | --- |
| informative under `.op` | 92 |
| informative under AC | 51 |
| under both | 19 |
| **under either** | **124** |
| added by AC alone | 32 |

These are *added* circuits, not rescued ones. 22 circuits are **refused**: they
contain a nonlinear element and their DC operating point does not solve, so
ngspice would linearise about whatever its fallback pinned. It prints a full node
table regardless, which is exactly why the test is made on the netlist and not on
whether output appeared.

Over the 19 circuits both probes can see, 13 are exact under both,
0 under `.op` alone and 3 under AC alone (exact McNemar
p = 0.250). The overlap is small, so this is not evidence that the probes
are interchangeable -- it is evidence that neither systematically contradicts the
other where both apply.

## Controls

`--self-test` asserts that a deck compared against itself scores exactly 1.000
under all three conditions, and that the AC parser excludes the sweep variable
and branch currents, computes magnitude as |re + j*im|, and returns an empty
result rather than a false zero when nothing parsed. That last one is not
hypothetical: this metric previously shipped a `print all` parse that was
silently wrong for every single-node deck.

## What this does not do

No condition applies `rshunt` or gmin stepping to make a singular deck solve.
Those voltages are an artefact of the fallback pinning a floating node, and
recovering circuits that way would manufacture agreement out of numerical
scaffolding.
