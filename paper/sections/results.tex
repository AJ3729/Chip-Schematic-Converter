\subsection{Detection (M1)}
Detection is essentially solved on this dataset:
\DetMapFifty{} mAP@0.5 (mean of three seeds; std \DetMapFiftyStd)
and \DetMapFiftyNinetyFive{} mAP@0.5:0.95.
Table~\ref{tab:detection} gives per-class AP with supports, and
Fig.~\ref{fig:per-class-ap} shows where the remaining variation is: AP@0.5
is saturated for every one of the \NumClasses{} classes, so what separates
them is localization tightness, not whether the symbol is found at all.

\subsection{Where the Error Is: Oracle Stage Attribution}
Substituting one stage at a time with ground truth localizes the loss
rather than inferring it. Table~\ref{tab:oracle} and
Fig.~\ref{fig:oracle} give the four modes: replacing detections buys
\OracleDetection{} terminal-pair $F_1$, replacing connectivity buys
\OracleWires, and replacing snapping buys \OracleSnapping. Wire
extraction owns an order of magnitude more error than either neighbour,
which is what motivates Sec.~\ref{sec:discussion}.

\subsection{Strict Success Is a Step Function of Precision}
Strict success is not spread across the test set --- it is concentrated
above a terminal-pair precision threshold and is identically zero below
it (Fig.~\ref{fig:cliff}). Every circuit that the pipeline solves
completely sits in the top precision bucket, on both splits, and the
buckets beneath it convert at zero despite holding most of the corpus.
\draftnote{this is the paper's most transferable finding --- say plainly
that it was found on the selection split and reproduced on data that never
influenced a parameter, and that it makes the objective ``move specific
circuits across one boundary'', not ``lift an average''.}

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{figures/fig_precision_cliff}
  \caption{Strict success rate by terminal-pair precision bucket, on both
  splits. Annotations are solved/total per bucket. The conversion rate is
  exactly zero everywhere below \BucketTopEdge{} --- not merely low --- and the shape
  reproduces on the split that never influenced a parameter.}
  \label{fig:cliff}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Repair: Solvability Lift with Zero Topology Cost (C5)}
Table~\ref{tab:repair} summarizes the repair layer. DC solvability
rises from \RepSolvBefore{} to \RepSolvAfter{} — a paired per-image
lift with 95\% CI $[\RepLiftCiLo, \RepLiftCiHi]$ and
\RepRegressed{} circuits made worse — at \RepMeanAssumptions{} logged
assumptions per circuit (\RepMeanGauge{} gauge entries). The integrity
rule holds experimentally as well as by construction:
\RepTopoViolations{} of \RepVerifiedImages{} circuits had any component
net assignment altered by repair.

Where the ledger's inferences can be checked against the verified
ground truth, they are. When a GND symbol is present, the reference net
the pipeline selects is the human's ground net in \GroundAccResolved{}
of decidable cases ($n=\GroundNGnd$; \GroundAccStrict{} if the
structurally ambiguous cases are counted as failures rather than
excluded). When no GND symbol is drawn, the ``most connected net''
fallback never matched the human's choice — a small sample, but a clean
negative result that says the fallback is a placeholder, not a method.

\subsection{Failure Cases}
Fig.~\ref{fig:gallery} shows one circuit per failure mode against its
ground truth. The circuits are selected by per-image metric --- the most
extreme precision/recall asymmetry, the most unmatched components, the
largest strict success --- not chosen by eye, so the gallery is not a
curated best case.

The over-merge row is the failure mode in its clearest form: the ground
truth carries fourteen distinct nets and the prediction assigns every
component to one. Terminal-pair recall is perfect on that circuit and
precision collapses, which is the signature the aggregate statistics
describe --- the conductors are all found, and then fused.
\draftnote{tie each row to the stage the oracle blames. Also note the
